Zenthos Gym

Membership, check-in, renewals and training for gyms running one or many branches.

Features

- Member app with membership status, days remaining and renewals
- Workouts, free for every member: a library of 1,283 exercises with anatomy animations, muscle filters and step-by-step instructions
- Routines members build themselves, plus ready-made workouts the gym publishes under Explore
- Live workout logging with last session's numbers, warm-up sets, a rest timer and boxing-bell sounds
- Rest alerts and a workout-in-progress notification that reach the phone even with the app closed
- Personal bests detected on the server, a celebration screen with share, and a progress calendar and charts
- Entrance QR check-in with distinct sounds for active, expired and repeat scans
- Live front desk with instant alerts, scoped to each branch
- Members list with photos, cash and transfer payments, password resets
- Renewals due list so members are contacted before they lapse
- Referral programme with invite links or an invite username at sign-up, and automatic rewards
- Admin control of branches, plans, pricing, staff and gym settings
- Card payment with Paystack, or bank transfer confirmed at the desk
- Push notifications, even when the app is closed
- Installable app with its own icon, and a one-swipe switch between the member app and the staff dashboard

Tech stack

- Next.js, React, TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase (Postgres, auth, realtime, storage, cron)
- Paystack
- ExerciseDB
